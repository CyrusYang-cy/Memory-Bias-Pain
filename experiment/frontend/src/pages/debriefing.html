Study Title: Understanding Judgments of People, Places, and Objects

Thank you for your participation. We would now like to tell you a little more about the study.

Purpose of the Study:

In this study, you were asked to view a series of faces one at a time and then reproduce them from memory. Specifically, the faces you saw varied in terms of their emotional expression. Here we are interested in what kinds of errors people make along emotion memory.

Past research shows that our memory is not perfectly accurate. For example, you may remember some faces as being “smilier” than they actually were, while others may be recalled as looking more “sad” than they truly were. But how should we interpret this memory error?

Here are a couple possibilities: 1) The inconsistency may reflect random noise in memory encoding. 2) The false memory may reflect a systematic memory bias caused by specific facial features of the given identity. This study will be informative about different kinds of face memory biases in psychological research.

As this study is ongoing, we ask that you not discuss this study with anyone else who is currently participating or might participate at a future time.
